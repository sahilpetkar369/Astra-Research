#📝 Project Overview
Astra Research — AI-Powered Scientific Research Platform
Astra Research (astra.research) is a futuristic, next-generation research workspace and intelligence layer designed to help AI/ML students, researchers, and engineers go from complex questions to clear, evidence-grounded insights. It bridges the gap between endless literature searches and rigorous scientific writing by surfacing relevant papers, mapping key evidence, and verifying citations in a unified environment.

# Key Features
Intelligent Research Workspace: Move seamlessly from initial question formulation to deep literature synthesis and note-taking.

Paper Discovery & Library: Search across scientific literature with contextual relevance scoring (e.g., NeurIPS, ICLR) and filter through saved literature without opening endless browser tabs.

Source-Linked Evidence Mapping: Automatically link insights, summaries, and notes back to their original source papers to ensure absolute accuracy and transparency.

Method & Approach Comparison: Compare competing methodologies, spot structural limitations, and turn findings into testable research directions.

Futuristic Dark-Themed UI: Crafted with a deep navy background (#070B1D), electric violet accents (#7957FF), mint green verified evidence badges (#70F0D0), and sleek charcoal-navy cards (#171D33).
